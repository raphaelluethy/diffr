## File icons: changes

The icons of @pierre/trees ship as one sprite. `crates/fileicon/tools/gen.py`
writes each as an SVG file of its own, unchanged otherwise, and turns the
package's lookup tables (file names, extensions, colors) into Rust tables;
names with no icon fall back to the default icon.

Many of these icons, and `crates/app/assets/icons/file/claude.svg` and its
like, depict the logos of languages, tools and companies. Those names and
logos are trademarks of their owners; diffr uses them only to mark the kind
of a file, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.
